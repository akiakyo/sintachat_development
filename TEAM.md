# The SintaChat Team

SintaChat is built and run by Computer Engineering students at the **Polytechnic University of the
Philippines**, specializing in Software and Network Engineering. Nicknames only, on purpose: real
names attached to an anonymous chat service would invite the harassment SintaChat exists to
prevent.

The live roster is at [team.sintachat.com](https://team.sintachat.com).
